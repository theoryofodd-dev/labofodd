Lab of Odd - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This private application is used only by the Lab of Odd team to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account.

What we access

When the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login, we receive an access token and the basic profile of that account (display name and avatar). We use it only to upload our videos and to read the status and view counts of our own posts.

What we store

The access token is stored on the account owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person, and we do not use the data for advertising.

Deletion

The account owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. We delete the stored token on request.
